# The Glass Coast

She arrived at the shore at dusk, her lantern the only light for miles.
The water was perfectly still.

# The Sunken Observatory

Below the surface, the dome still glowed faintly with the residue of old
instruments. She had read about it in three different accounts, none of
them agreeing on what lay inside.

# The Road East

The amber road began at the edge of the bay and did not end anywhere
she had found on a map.
